First-year internship: training an AI for waste recognition

Paprec, R&D team · June 15 – July 10, 2026 (4 weeks) · AI Waste Manager project

Context

The AI Waste Manager project aims to predict the combustion and polluting emissions of waste before it reaches the furnace of an incineration plant, using a camera and an artificial intelligence capable of recognizing the type of waste.

My role

  • Manual segmentation and categorization of waste (plastic, cardboard, metal…) on photographs of the hopper with Label Studio, to build the AI training dataset.
  • Development of a Python script (PyCharm) that frees up disk space by automatically deleting some older images when storage space is low.
  • Transfer and organization of the plants' action lists from Excel to Microsoft Planner, then Microsoft Lists, for the Operational Excellence team.

First-year project: floating wave attenuator for the Port of Dunkirk

Small-scale experimental study · team project of 11 students, for Terres Océanes · 2025-2026

Context

The Port of Dunkirk is well protected from swell, but remains exposed to short waves (chop) created by wind and maritime traffic. We evaluated floating wave attenuator models at small scale in a wave flume, respecting Froude similarity, at high tide and at low tide.

My role

  • Operation of the wave flume and data acquisition.
  • Probe calibration: calculation of the calibration coefficients in Matlab from the measurements, then checking their consistency before sharing them with the team.
  • Data analysis (amplitudes, damping coefficients) and writing of the report in LaTeX.
